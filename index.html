MEASURE LAB

six original browser experiments in computer vision, games, and music.
inspired by the work of @measure_plan (hand tracking, sports cv, webcam toys, generative art).
everything runs on-device. no accounts, no uploads, no dependencies beyond a cdn.
game / sports cv
TOPSPIN
a tennis rally played inside a computer-vision annotation feed. the model calls your forehands, clocks ball speed, and logs every stroke to the session db.
game / webcam motion
METEOR SHIELD
your body is the controller. dependency-free motion tracking slides an energy shield that swats meteors away from a city skyline.
gesture instrument
ORBITAL
a particle nebula you conduct with your hands. left hand attracts, right hand repels, pinch fires a shockwave and plays a note.
music toy
SEQUENCE SCAPE
draw a landscape, drop balls on it, and the terrain plays itself. pitch is where they land, loudness is how hard they fall.
video art
SIGNAL DECAY
webcam or any image rendered as a phosphor ascii terminal with datamosh trails, scanlines, and character corruption.
image tool
SORTING HAT
an animated pixel-sorting glitch lab. upload any photo and watch it endlessly re-sort into ribbons. export the frame you love.
built with canvas, webaudio/tone.js and optional mediapipe - each project is one index.html. mouse/touch fallbacks everywhere a camera is used.
